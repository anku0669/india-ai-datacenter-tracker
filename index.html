India's AI Data Center Boom — Tracked Live

India is in the middle of the largest data-center build-out in its history. Hyperscalers, conglomerates and state governments have announced well over US $150 billion in new AI infrastructure since 2025 — from Reliance's 1.5 GW cluster in Visakhapatnam to Adani's US $100 billion, 2035 roadmap. This page tracks where that capacity is landing, how much power and water it consumes against India's total national resources, what happens if the country keeps building, and who bears the cost.

Inspired by and modeled after the U.S. community-awareness project Brockovich Data Center Reporting, which maps AI data centers and citizen concerns across the United States. This is an independent, India-focused adaptation built from public data — not affiliated with that project.

About This Tracker

This page is an independent, open-source effort to do for India what the Brockovich Data Center Reporting project has done for the United States: put the scattered, hard-to-find facts about AI infrastructure — who is building what, where, how big, and at what environmental cost — onto a single, continuously updatable page that any citizen, journalist, policymaker or investor can check. It is not affiliated with any government body, operator, or the original Brockovich project.

Methodology: every figure on this page is sourced from public disclosures — company press releases, stock-exchange filings, sector reports from KPMG and SBI, Ministry of Power / CEA data, Central Water Commission assessments, state government incentive notifications, and investigative journalism from outlets including Down To Earth, Eco-Business, Scroll.in and The Hindu BusinessLine. The dataset is maintained as plain, human-readable code (data.js) on GitHub, so anyone can audit, fork, or submit a correction.

Timeline of Major Announcements (2025-2026)

Early 2025 — AWS commits $8.3 billion to expand its Mumbai region, signaling the start of an accelerated multi-year investment cycle.

December 2025 — AWS pledges an additional $7 billion for Hyderabad; Microsoft announces a $17.5 billion AI infrastructure investment spanning Hyderabad and Pune.

Early-to-mid 2026 — AWS discloses a $430 million data center near Navi Mumbai (Taloja) and pledges a further $13 billion through 2030, bringing its cumulative India commitment to roughly $48 billion. Google announces a $15 billion, 1 GW campus in Visakhapatnam alongside three new subsea cable systems.

June 2026 — Reliance Industries and Meta announce a 168 MW, build-to-suit AI data center in Jamnagar, Gujarat — Meta's first dedicated facility in India, notable for its desalinated-seawater cooling design.

Mid-2026 — Reliance unveils its flagship 1.5 GW, three-phase Visakhapatnam cluster (~$17-19 billion), positioning it as potentially India's single largest data center investment, with Phase 1 targeted for commercial operation by October 2028.

August 2026 — Adani Group discloses plans for a $100 billion, decade-long (through 2035) roadmap to scale AdaniConneX from 2 GW to roughly 5 GW of national capacity, alongside a proposed ₹1 trillion, 1 GW facility in Odisha. In the same window, community pushback surfaces publicly in Thane over Amazon's Balkum facility, and legal and civil-society groups intensify calls to suspend approvals for undisclosed water sourcing in the Vizag cluster.
